Article VI · Mission

Why "sovereign" is the operative word

Most AI tools ask an organization to send its data somewhere else to get value back. Cantek's position is that the model should come to the data instead — deployed on infrastructure the client already controls, governed by the client's own policies, and auditable on demand.

Value

Ownership

The organization keeps its data, its models, and its decisions in-house.

Value

Rigor

Governance frameworks and real audits, not a demo that never ships.

Value

Fit

Systems built against the client's actual stack, not a generic template.
